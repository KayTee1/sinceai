Problem & idea

Self-service BI and AI make dashboards nearly free, so enterprises drown in duplicates, conflicting KPIs, unowned reports and tech debt. Bayer’s Tableau→Power BI migration makes it worse.

Our answer: agents that reuse before they build, flag KPI conflicts, build only temporary insights on certified KPIs, and make every asset re-earn its place every 90 days. Agents propose; humans decide; silence has a logged, reversible default; everything is audited.

No response = archive by default — logged as a policy action, expiry warning first, one-click restore, and never for assets with downstream dependencies or no owner (those go to review). Renew keeps the tier. Simulated clock: 1 day = 1 second (30-day TTL = 30 s, 90-day lease = 90 s); god mode pauses, resumes and fast-forwards, never rewinds. Tiers are app-owned; Bayer’s source fields stay untouched.

Request flow

  1. Manager runs a request for a named requester (e.g. REQ-0001).
  2. Orchestrator parses intent; asks when text and context disagree.
  3. Discovery: hybrid search over assets, KPIs, models.
  4. Conflict detector: e.g. Net Sales vs Net Sales Local vs v2.
  5. Fits → reuse certified asset (usage +1).
  6. Nothing fits → approval → Temporary insight on certified KPIs only, with expiry.

Stretch goal (build if time remains): data-source health checks

Scheduled cron jobs check every source: arrival (did refreshes run on schedule, missed or late?), freshness (is the data older than its limit?) and validity (schema or volume changes, quality rules, inactive owner, closed or restated periods). Health flows down the lineage so dashboards on stale data get a warning badge; unhealthy sources block certification and raise an owner proposal. Policy windows per domain — finance period close, legal retention and holds, HR retention / GDPR, GxP — with every check logged as audit evidence. After P0/P1, before Sat 22:00 freeze: small version on the simulated clock using the pack's refresh and quality fields. Supports compliance with evidence; doesn't certify it.
